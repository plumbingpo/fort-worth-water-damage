Common Water Damage Issues in San Antonio

Water usually escapes at the same few vulnerable spots in a home. These are the ones San Antonio property owners report to us again and again.

Water heaters: Sediment collects in the bottom of a tank and slowly eats through the steel. The first sign is often a rusty puddle or a damp ring on the floor around the base. A split tank does not stop at one tankful, because the inlet keeps feeding it until the valve is shut. Heaters in a hallway closet or attic can ruin flooring and ceilings well beyond the utility area.

Roofs: Wind can lift shingles and drive rain underneath them in a single storm. Skylights, plumbing vents and chimneys are other entry points once their seals crack with age. Since water follows the framing downhill, the ceiling stain rarely sits directly below the actual opening. For roof leaks in San Antonio, we tarp where needed, pull out soaked insulation and dry the structure so the ceiling can be repaired.

Freezing: Cold nights, depending on the season, can break plumbing that showed no sign of trouble earlier. The first to go are hose bibs, irrigation lines and any pipe routed through an unheated space. Vacant homes and rooms with the heat turned down are especially exposed. No flow or a bare trickle at the tap on a frigid day means a frozen line, so close the main and get help.

Backups: Gurgling sounds and more than one slow fixture at once suggest the main drain is partly blocked. When the clog becomes complete, every flush and every load of laundry comes back out downstairs. Since the waste is hazardous, we extract it and then sanitize concrete, tile and other hard surfaces. Until a plumber opens the line, do not use sinks or toilets and keep everyone clear of the mess.

Seepage: Dampness at the base of a basement or foundation wall often means water is collecting outside. The usual reasons are grading that tilts inward, clogged perimeter drains and downspouts that empty too close. Early clues include powdery white residue on the concrete, flaking wall paint and air that always smells musty. The team at Fort Worth Restoration can extract standing water and dry the space, and we will tell you what we think let it in.
